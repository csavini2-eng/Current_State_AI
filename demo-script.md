# Weenstone demo script

Approximately 2½ minutes. Read the narration; bracketed directions are actions, not spoken text. Use synthetic information and prepare one incomplete sample milestone before recording. If you complete the personal-upload test before recording, update the final limitation to reflect the actual result.

[Show My Dogs.]

Hi, I’m Catherine, and this is Weenstone.

Weenstone helps trainers and handlers follow the same training plan for a dog. The problem we identified is that different people may use different cues and methods. Our goal is to make the trainer’s instructions clear and keep everyone informed about progress.

[Open a sample dog and its Foundation Skills journey.]

The trainer can create a dog profile and assign a training path. Each milestone shows its status and what successful completion should look like.

When I open View instructions, I can see the agreed cue, the objective, and the practice instructions.

[Open Sit instructions.]

One important revision came directly from peer feedback. A reviewer pointed out that “Details to be added” did not help them understand when to mark a skill completed. We replaced that placeholder with clear objectives and instructions.

We also made these instructions specific to each dog. Changing one dog’s cue should not change another dog’s plan. Our assisted checks confirmed that separation for two sample dogs.

[Close instructions. Click Start practicing, then Mark completed on an incomplete sample.]

We simplified the progress controls and added a completion message. The counter updates after the change is saved.

[Open Sit, then Videos & practice, Optional bundled Sit example, and Try sample feedback.]

The intended AI feature would help handlers review their practice between sessions.

This version demonstrates that experience through prepared feedback for a sample video. It is clearly labeled as simulated. It does not automatically analyze the recording.

The feedback explains what the example shows and what cannot be confirmed. I can edit the note, save it, or dismiss it. These actions do not automatically complete a milestone, which keeps the decision under human control.

[Show the handler page using a newly created synthetic sample link.]

The handler link makes the dog’s assigned plan and progress accessible without an account. The trainer can revoke the link.

Our checks support the basic planning and progress flow, but they do not prove training effectiveness or AI accuracy. Personal-video submission still needs an end-to-end check.

My next priority is to complete that check and ask a trainer or handler whether the feedback helps them decide what to practice next.

## Recording and submission

Make a 10-second test to check audio and readable text, then record a 2–3 minute draft. Upload the video to Google Drive, grant the two required viewers access, add its viewing link under Demo Video in README, and upload the same video file directly to Canvas together with the repository URL. A script is not a substitute for the recording.
